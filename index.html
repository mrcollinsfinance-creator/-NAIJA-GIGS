📖 How Naija Gigs Works

Naija Gigs is designed to make it easier for people in Nigeria to connect around small jobs and services.

1

🔎 Find a Gig

Browse jobs by category, location, or search for a specific opportunity.

4

✅ Complete the Job

Complete the agreed work and receive your payment.

🛡️ Safety & Scam Awareness

Please remember that Naija Gigs is a platform for connecting people. Always use your judgment before accepting a job or sending money.


Never send money just because someone promises you a job.


Verify the person, business, job details, location and payment terms before starting.


If something feels suspicious, do not continue with the transaction.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Naija Gigs free?

Yes. The basic platform is designed to be free to use.

Can I post a job?

Yes. Tap "Post a Gig", complete the form and publish your job.

How do I contact someone?

Where a WhatsApp number has been provided, you can contact the gig poster through WhatsApp.

Does Naija Gigs guarantee a job?

No. Naija Gigs connects people. Users are responsible for agreeing on the job, payment and other details.

Can I use Naija Gigs from anywhere in Nigeria?

Yes. Gigs can be posted with different Nigerian locations.

🔐 Privacy

Naija Gigs is being developed in stages. At this stage, gig information entered on this version is stored locally in the user's browser.


Do not enter passwords, bank PINs, card details, BVN, OTPs or other highly sensitive information into a gig description.


When the shared database is introduced, the privacy system will be updated to explain what information is stored and how it is used.

📋 Terms

Users are responsible for the accuracy of information they post.


Do not post illegal jobs, fraudulent offers, stolen goods, harmful content or misleading information.


Users should verify jobs, workers, businesses, prices and payment arrangements before entering into an agreement.


Naija Gigs is a connection platform and does not currently guarantee transactions between users.
