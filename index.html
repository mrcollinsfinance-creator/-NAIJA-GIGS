🛡️ Stay Safe

  • Never send money to someone just to get a job.
  • Discuss the work, payment and expectations clearly.
  • Meet in a safe public place when appropriate.
  • Do not share passwords, PINs or sensitive banking information.
  • Report suspicious or fraudulent gigs.

About Naija Gigs

A marketplace for everyday opportunities.

Naija Gigs is designed to make it easier for people in Nigeria to discover opportunities and connect with people who need their skills. From delivery and repairs to design, tutoring, cleaning and online work, the goal is simple: make finding useful work easier.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Naija Gigs free?

Yes. The marketplace is being built with a free-first approach.

Do I need an account?

You can browse available gigs, but an account is required for account-based actions such as posting and applying.

Can I use Naija Gigs from another phone?

Yes. Your account is stored in the online Supabase system. Log in with the same email and password.

Does Naija Gigs guarantee payment?

No. Users should agree on payment terms themselves and use caution when dealing with strangers.
